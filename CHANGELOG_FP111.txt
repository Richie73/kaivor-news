FP111: One refresh button, following the view
================================================
Date: 16 Nov 2026. Richie's question: "Would it make sense to have
the settings gear, the refresh market and refresh news buttons in
the same place?"

What was wrong: three refresh buttons overlapped. The news button
sat in the section-title row, which stays visible in every view, so
it showed even in Chat. The market panel had its own "Refresh
markets" button. The Markets view had a third "Refresh" button —
so the Markets view showed all three at once.

The fix (agreed with Richie): one button in the title row,
id="refresh-btn", onclick refreshCurrentView(this). updateRefreshButton()
sets its label and visibility from the view state:
  - Markets view open -> "Refresh markets", visible.
  - News views -> "Refresh news", visible.
  - Weather, Hub, or a container view (Saved, Chat, Settings,
    Receipts, RAMS) -> hidden.
refreshCurrentView dispatches to refreshMarketsFromView (prices plus
the five-day history, as before) in Markets, else to
refreshNewsFromButton. Both refresh functions now restore the
button's label through updateRefreshButton() instead of hardcoding
it, so a view change mid-refresh cannot mislabel the button.
Settings stays in the header — it is global.

Call sites for updateRefreshButton(): filterCategory,
cacheNewsContainer (after the early return), restoreNewsContainer,
showWeather, showMarkets, showMorningHub. The two old buttons and
the #market-refresh CSS rules are removed.

Tests: 8 in tests/test_unified_refresh_fp111.py (markup, old
buttons gone, Settings in the header, call sites, restore paths,
history reload, and two node tests — button states across six view
combinations and the dispatch). FP095's markup test and FP086's
markets-section test updated for the new button. Local: 880 passed
