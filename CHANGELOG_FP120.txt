FP120 — Softer brand green (leaf green #3aa76d)
================================================

Richie found the Spark K neon #35FA33 too bright, and picked
leaf green #3aa76d as the most polished of three softer
options (A #4ade80 soft, B #3aa76d leaf, C #2e8b57 muted —
shown to him as a swatch widget on the app's black
background). The swap covers every place the old green lived:

- templates/index.html: the six accent tokens
  (--brand/--green/--green-bright/--green-soft/--green-pale/
  --green-dark) are #3aa76d; the market flash glow is
  #3aa76d66; the splash logo rect is #3aa76d; the markets
  sparkline up-stroke is '#3aa76d'.
- templates/reading.html: --accent and --brand are #3aa76d.
- The near-black green tints (--green-panel, --green-border,
  --btn-bg, --btn-border, --green-darker, --on-brand and the
  muted text greens) were KEPT from FP117: at that darkness
  the hue difference from the old 120-degree tints is
  imperceptible, so re-tinting would have been churn with no
  visible effect. --on-brand (#071207, dark text on bright
  green buttons) still reads fine on #3aa76d (~5:1).
- Logo: the SVG source is recolored and renamed to
  kaivor-logo-v4.svg; the PNG icons are rebuilt as v4
  (192 + 512) from the SVG geometry with Pillow (4x
  supersample, LANCZOS downscale) — pixel-verified at build
  time: background corner (58,167,109) = #3aa76d, K stem and
  spark black. New filenames so Android picks the new icon
  up from the new URLs (same-name icon changes get stuck in
  the Android icon cache — the FP083 lesson). The v3 files
  are deleted. Manifest, service worker cache list, favicon
  and both header brand-marks move to v4. The orphan v2 PNGs
  were already unreferenced and are left alone.

Tests: 4 new in tests/test_softer_green_fp120.py (no #35FA33
left in either template, the SVG, the manifest or the worker;
brand tokens are #3aa76d; splash/sparkline/flash use it; v4
icons exist at the right sizes and v3 is gone — PNG sizes
from the headers, no Pillow in the phone suite). Updated:
tests/test_one_green_fp117.py (BRAND + allowed set moved to
#3aa76d, #35FA33 added to OLD_GREENS so it can never come
back, the tints test renamed to reflect the kept tints),
tests/test_design_system_fp090.py (token values + the banned
literal), tests/test_logo_splash_fp083.py (v4 everywhere),
tests/test_markets_view_fp086.py (spark stroke),
tests/test_rename_fp116.py (manifest icon src).
