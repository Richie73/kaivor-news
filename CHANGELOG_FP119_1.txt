FP119.1 — Rain radar zoom fix
==============================

The bug
-------
Richie's screenshot (18 Nov 05:49) showed the radar overlay
covered in "Zoom Level Not Supported" tiles. Diagnosis, by
fetching RainViewer tiles directly: the free radar tiles only
exist up to zoom 7. Zooms 5, 6 and 7 return real radar tiles
(18–28 KB, different content per zoom); zooms 8–12 all return
the SAME 1370-byte placeholder PNG at every location. The map
was initialised at zoom 8 — one level too far in — so every
tile was the placeholder.

The fix
-------
- Default zoom 8 → 7 (RAIN_RADAR_ZOOM).
- The Leaflet map is capped at minZoom 5 / maxZoom 7, so
  pinch or scroll can never reach the unsupported zooms;
  the + button greys out at 7.
- The radar overlay gets maxNativeZoom: 7 as belt and
  braces — Leaflet upscales the z7 tiles rather than
  requesting tiles that do not exist.

At zoom 7 the map shows roughly the whole of England and
Wales, so rain heading for the Wirral from the Irish Sea
is still visible.

Verification lesson
-------------------
The FP119 build check fetched a tile and confirmed only
status 200 + image/png — the placeholder is also a 200 PNG.
Tile checks must compare content (size/hash), not just
status. This one is now covered by the zoom test below.
