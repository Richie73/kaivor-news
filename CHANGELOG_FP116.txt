FP116 — The app is called Kaivor now
====================================

News is one of six things on the bottom bar — chat, memory,
tasks, RAMS and receipts sit next to it — so "Kaivor News"
undersold what the app is. The app is now just Kaivor, with
the tagline "Your personal AI".

- templates/index.html: <title> Kaivor; header brand KAIVOR
  with sub-header "Your personal AI"; splash word KAIVOR with
  tag "Your personal AI"; Settings About line says Kaivor.
- templates/reading.html: brand KAIVOR (the page keeps its own
  title "Kaivor Reading" and sub "Reading Intelligence"); the
  PIN setup hint says "the Kaivor environment settings".
- static/manifest.webmanifest: name and short_name "Kaivor",
  description "Your personal AI — news, chat, memory, tasks
  and more." The home-screen app name updates from this — the
  app may need removing and re-adding from the home screen
  for the new name to show (same as the icon change in FP083).
- main.py: the AI brief system prompt says "You are Kaivor."
- confidence_aware_ai.py: the analyst prompt says "You are
  Kaivor's cautious evidence-grounded news analyst."
- tests/test_logo_splash_fp083.py: splash assertions updated.

Deliberately unchanged: the icon filenames (kaivor-news-*.png),
the web address (kaivor-news.onrender.com), the service worker
cache name, and module docstrings (historical FP references,
not user-visible).

Tests: 8 in tests/test_rename_fp116.py (title, header, splash,
About line, manifest JSON, Reading brand, no old branding left
in the user-facing files, AI prompts).
