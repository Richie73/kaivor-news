FP128 — No stock photos on puzzle cards
=====================================

Why
---
20 Nov 2026: the Wordle card showed a water-park photo of kids on
inflatable toys (Eva Rinaldi · CC BY-SA 2.0). The FP089 fallback
guessed wrong: for the title "The New York Times - Wordle Daily
Challenge" the single-word fallback query is "Challenge" (the
longest distinctive word), Openverse returns challenge-tagged
photos, and "Puzzles" is ungated so one keyword hit passes.

Puzzle cards are links to puzzle apps, not stories — a guessed
photo can only misfire, so they get no stock photos at all.

Changes
-------
- stock_images.py: _NO_STOCK_CATEGORIES = {"puzzles"};
  get_stock_image returns None for the category before any
  lookup (case-insensitive). News categories are unaffected.
- templates/index.html, both card renderers: the Jinja card
  uses {% elif article.category != 'Puzzles' %} instead of a
  plain {% else %} for the placeholder; the JS card renders ''
  for Puzzles instead of the placeholder div.
- tests/test_stock_images_fp089.py: test_placeholder_in_jinja_card
  updated to the new elif string (same milestone, exact-rule
  assertion).

Files
-----
- stock_images.py
- templates/index.html
- tests/test_stock_images_fp089.py (one assertion updated)
- tests/test_puzzle_no_stock_photo_fp128.py (7 tests)

Tests: local targeted 7 passed; local full 1100 passed /
11 known failures / 2 skipped.

Phone-side proof: open the Puzzles view — the Wordle card
(and every other puzzle card) shows no photo at all, just
the title, description and Open in app button.
