FP076 — More puzzles in the Puzzles category
==========================================

Background (13 Nov 2026)
- Richie asked for more puzzles. The Puzzles category held only 4
  link cards (NYT Wordle, Guardian crosswords, Independent
  crosswords & Sudoku, NYT Mini Crossword). This milestone takes it
  to 12. The category is links out to the publishers' puzzle pages
  — no puzzles are hosted inside Kaivor itself.

What was built
- 8 new entries in puzzle_items() (main.py), all free to play with
  no account needed for the daily puzzle:
  - NYT Connections — group 16 words into 4 sets.
  - NYT Strands — find the hidden theme words in a letter grid.
  - Quordle — 4 Wordles at once in 9 guesses.
  - Nerdle — Wordle with numbers; guess the calculation in 6 tries.
  - Sudoku.com — daily Sudoku, easy to extreme.
  - Lichess puzzles — chess puzzles from real games, rated to level.
  - Worldle — guess the country from its outline.
  - Waffle — swap letters to make six words in 15 moves.
- Every URL was checked live before building: 6 via a page fetch
  (Quordle, Nerdle, Sudoku.com, Lichess, Worldle, Waffle) and the
  2 NYT pages via the live browser (nytimes.com blocks the fetch
  tool). All 8 loaded the right game. The Connections page shows a
  "Subscribe for unlimited play" message — the daily puzzle itself
  is free, as with Wordle.

Tests
- 4 new tests in tests/test_puzzles_fp076.py: 12 entries with all
  8 new titles present; every entry complete (Puzzles category,
  title, description, source, "Daily", https link); links and
  titles unique.
- Full suite (local, test-only secrets stub): see README_INSTALL_FP076.txt.
- Phone suite after install: see README_INSTALL_FP076.txt.

Out of scope (unchanged): playable puzzles inside Kaivor itself
(each would be its own mini-app — a possible later milestone);
the 4 original puzzle links; ROADMAP.md; Render deploy (follows
the normal push flow after the Termux install, with a manual
dashboard deploy if auto-deploy does not fire — it did not fire
for the FP071 push on 13 Nov).
