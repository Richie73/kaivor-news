Kaivor FP089 — Stock photo fallback for imageless articles
========================================================

WHAT CHANGED (third of the polish pass)
---------------------------------------
About a third of stories have no publisher picture (FP075 found 410
of 656 with images). Imageless cards now get a matching free stock
photo, marked as illustrative so it never looks like the actual
event photo.

HOW IT WORKS
------------
- LAZY, not at refresh time. The refresh already takes ~90 seconds;
  adding per-article lookups there would slow every cycle and hit
  Openverse's anonymous limit (20/min). Instead the card renders a
  placeholder and the photo is fetched only when the card scrolls
  into view — naturally paced by human scrolling.
- stock_images.py (new): keywords_from_headline pulls up to five
  picture-worthy words from the headline (stopwords and short
  words dropped); _fetch_from_openverse asks the Openverse API
  (api.openverse.org, no key needed) for one commercially
  licensed image; results are cached per normalised query
  (200 entries max, oldest evicted; failures retried after 60s).
  Nothing here ever raises — any failure returns None and the
  card stays text-only.
- GET /api/stock_image?q=<headline> (open GET, like the finance
  history): returns {success, image_url, credit, query} or
  {success:false}. A missing/overlong headline is a 400.
- Template: the Jinja card and the Saved render both show a
  shimmer placeholder for imageless articles. An
  IntersectionObserver (200px root margin; plain immediate load
  where the API is missing) calls loadStockPhoto, which swaps in
  the image plus a caption line "Illustrative photo · <creator>
  · <licence>" and marks the card (data-image-illustrative,
  data-image-credit). A broken stock image removes itself and
  the caption. Placeholders are re-observed after the Saved view
  renders and after the news container is restored.
- Saving a card carries the flag and credit through
  articleFromCard and normalise_article (two new keys), so the
  Saved view shows the same "Illustrative photo" marking.
