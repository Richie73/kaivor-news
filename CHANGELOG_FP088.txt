Kaivor FP088 — Loading states: skeleton screens
==============================================

WHAT CHANGED (second of the polish pass)
----------------------------------------
1. NEWS. When the page loads with no stories — the cache is empty
   right after a deploy — the feed used to be blank until someone
   reloaded. Now three skeleton cards (shimmering grey shapes in
   the card layout, image block 16:9) show with a "Loading the
   latest stories…" note, and a small poll checks /api/health
   every 10 seconds and reloads the page as soon as stories
   exist. After 30 tries (5 minutes) it stops and the note says
   to reload by hand. The poll only runs when skeleton cards are
   on the page, so normal loads are untouched.
2. WEATHER. The strip and the full-view hero show skeleton shimmer
   while the forecast loads, instead of "Loading weather…" text.
   The error message ("Weather unavailable…") is unchanged.
3. MARKETS. The view opens with four skeleton rows instead of the
   "Loading market history…" line.
4. The shimmer is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion (added to
   the FP083 reduced-motion list).

TECHNICAL
---------
- templates/index.html only (CSS + markup + one poll script).
  No Python, no new routes, no keys, no cost.
- The news poll uses the existing open GET /api/health
  (news_count). The page load itself already triggers the
  background refresh when the cache is empty, so the poll just
  waits for it to finish.
