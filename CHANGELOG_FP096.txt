Kaivor FP096 — Type scale + spacing tidy-up
========================================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
Design system, part 2 (FP090 did colours and radii). The news
page had 15 distinct font sizes and 7 line-heights; both pages
now use shared tokens:

- Type: --text-xs 10, --text-sm 11, --text-base 12, --text-md 13,
  --text-lg 14, --text-xl 16, --text-2xl 20, --text-hero 48
  (Reading adds --text-display 26 for its headings and stats).
- Leading: --leading-none 1, --leading-snug 1.4,
  --leading-normal 1.5, --leading-relaxed 1.6.
- Space: --space-xs 4, --space-sm 8, --space-md 12, --space-lg 16.

Convergences (barely visible, all towards the nearest step):
9px badges -> 10px; 15/17px headings -> 16px; 1.38 -> 1.4;
1.45 -> 1.5; 1.65 -> 1.6; Reading h1 25px -> 26px.

Deliberately left hardcoded: the icon glyph sizes (weather 18px
and 44px, scroll-to-top 22px) and a few off-scale margins
(6/9/11px) and paddings.

Also: the article description's styling lived as an inline style
duplicated in both card renders (Jinja + Saved view JS) — it is
one .article-description rule now, so the renders cannot drift.
