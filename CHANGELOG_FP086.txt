Kaivor FP086 — Full Markets view + price history
===============================================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. FULL MARKETS VIEW. Tapping the Market Watch strip opens a full
   Markets view (like the weather view: it hides the news cards
   without destroying them, so tapping a news category brings the
   stories straight back). One row per asset:
   - current price (same value the ticker shows)
   - today's change: ▲/▼ with the amount and percentage, green/red
   - a five-trading-day sparkline (green when up over the period,
     red when down)
   - today's range (high–low) for Gold, Bitcoin, S&P 500 and Brent
   - the five-day range for GBP/USD, GBP/EUR and EUR/USD
     (Frankfurter carries no intraday range)
   A Refresh button re-runs the market refresh and reloads the
   history. #markets deep link supported.
2. PRICE HISTORY, NO STORAGE. History comes from the same free
   sources as the live prices — Yahoo Finance chart data
   (range=5d, interval=1d) for the four USD assets, Frankfurter
   timeseries for the three FX pairs — fetched on demand by a new
   GET /api/finance/history route and cached for 15 minutes. No
   migration, no new keys, no cost, nothing stored. Weekends and
   holidays have no rate, so a series holds however many trading
   days the window covered.
3. The current price prefers the stored Market Watch value (so the
   view agrees with the ticker) and falls back to the history
   fetch's latest price when the store is empty — so the Markets
   view shows prices even before Refresh markets is tapped. A
   failed fetch serves the stale cache when one exists.

TECHNICAL
---------
- main.py: _fetch_yahoo_history / _fetch_fx_history /
  get_finance_history + GET /api/finance/history. The route is a
  GET (public market data) so it stays open like the ticker.
- templates/index.html: the market bar is tappable (cursor
  pointer, "tap to open" label); #markets-section after the
  weather section; showMarkets/hideMarketsView mirror the
  weather view; hideMarketsView is called by filterCategory,
  showSaved, showChat, showSettings and showWeather. The pure
  helpers formatMarketPrice / formatMarketChange / sparklineSvg
  are node-tested.
- Both sources were verified live before building (Yahoo
  5-day chart for GC=F, Frankfurter 7-day timeseries).
