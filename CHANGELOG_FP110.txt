FP110: Live football scores (Premier League + England) in the Sport view
======================================================================
Date: 16 Nov 2026. Requested by Richie: live scores, as close to
instant as possible, for the English Premier League and the England
national team, plus the UK TV channel for each game.

What was verified first (16 Nov, live probes)
--------------------------------------------
- ESPN's free scoreboard feed works with no key:
  https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/soccer/{league}/scoreboard
  (200, CORS *, Premier League gameweek + Nations League fixtures
  returned with scores, clocks and states). It updates roughly every
  30-60 seconds during play — near-live, not truly instant. No free
  source is instant; genuinely instant feeds are paid data products.
- England appear across fifa.friendly, uefa.nations, fifa.worldq.uefa,
  uefa.euroq, fifa.world and uefa.euro; no England games were scheduled
  in the probe week, so the team-name filter is verified against other
  nations' fixtures, not a live England game.
- UK TV channels: NO free feed carries them. ESPN's broadcasts fields
  are empty; TheSportsDB's free key is blocked for fixtures. So the
  scores strip carries a "Where to watch on UK TV" link to
  live-footballontv.com instead of a channel name that might be wrong.

Design
------
- New module football_scores.py: fetches the 7 scoreboards in parallel
  (ThreadPoolExecutor, 10s timeout each), keeps the Premier League in
  full and England matches only from the other six, sorts live first
  then scheduled then finished, caches for 30 seconds (errors cached
  too, like travel/warnings). One league failing does not break the
  others; all failing returns status "error".
- Route GET /api/football/scores in main.py — open read (public data),
  returns {success, status, updated, premier_league, england,
  live_count}.
- Template: a #football-scores card between the status line and the
  news cards, shown only when the Sport category is active
  (updateFootballScores, called at the end of filterCategory).
  Hidden by hideFootballScores from cacheNewsContainer (covers Saved/
  Chat/Settings/Receipts/RAMS) and from showWeather/showMarkets/
  showMorningHub. While visible the page re-polls every 30s
  (FOOTBALL_SCORES_POLL_MS); the timer stops when the strip hides.
  Rows: live "● LIVE 67' 2–1", finished "FT 3–0", scheduled kickoff
  time (HH:MM, en-GB). The England section hides itself when there
  are no England fixtures. An "Updated HH:MM:SS" line proves it is
  live. No pulse animation (deliberate — avoids reduced-motion churn).
