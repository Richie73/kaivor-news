Kaivor FP075.2 — Sharper pictures: the other BBC image form
=========================================================

WHAT CHANGED (found in FP075.1's live check, 15 Nov 2026)
---------------------------------------------------------
FP075.1 upgraded 95 of the 96 BBC pictures to 1024px. One
card was left behind: it uses the BBC's newer image URL form,
  https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/images/ic/240x135/p0pmwlpj.jpg
which the FP075.1 rewrite did not cover.

HOW IT WORKS NOW
----------------
_clean_image_url in news_store.py now also rewrites the
/images/ic/<WxH>/ form:
  .../images/ic/240x135/... -> .../images/ic/1024x576/...
Verified live: the same image returns 1024x576 (150 KB).
