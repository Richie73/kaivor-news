Kaivor FP086.1 — Markets: fill the strip + four more rows
======================================================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. THE STRIP FILLS FROM THE VIEW. The home-page Market Watch strip
   reads the stored Market Watch data, which is empty until a
   PIN-gated refresh — so after the Markets view loaded, the strip
   still said "No live data yet". Now, when the Markets view loads,
   it also fills the strip's price spans from the same history data,
   and the status line reads "Prices from the Markets view ·
   Updated …" instead. The next real refresh (or the 30-second
   ticker poll finding stored data) takes over normally.
2. FOUR MORE ROWS. The Markets view now has eleven rows:
   - Silver (Yahoo SI=F) next to Gold
   - Ethereum (Yahoo ETH-USD) next to Bitcoin
   - FTSE 100 (Yahoo ^FTSE) next to the S&P 500
   - GBP/JPY (Frankfurter, rides the existing GBP call) with the
     other currency pairs
   All four were verified live before building. They are view-only:
   the live ticker, the stored Market Watch data and the chat hub
   stay at the original seven assets.

TECHNICAL
---------
- main.py: Silver/Ethereum/FTSE100 join the Yahoo symbol map;
  GBP/JPY joins the Frankfurter GBP call and _FINANCE_FX_KEYS;
  _FINANCE_HISTORY_LABELS holds the four new labels (the chat
  hub's _FINANCE_CHAT_ASSETS is untouched). History now makes 9
  requests per refresh (7 Yahoo + 2 Frankfurter).
- templates/index.html: fillTickerFromHistory(history) in the
  FP086 block calls updateMarketValue for each row's current
  price (the view-only assets have no ticker span, so they are
  ignored there) and stamps marketHistoryFill; the row order is
  metals, crypto, indices, oil, FX. updateFinanceTicker clears
  the flag when stored data exists and shows the "Prices from
  the Markets view" status while the store is empty.
- formatMarketPrice formats GBP/JPY to four decimals like the
  other FX pairs.
