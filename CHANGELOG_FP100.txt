Kaivor FP100 — RAMS drafting
============================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
Draft a Risk Assessment and Method Statement on the standard
template, from the "📋 Draft a RAMS" button in the Morning Hub.

- The user describes the task, site and plant, ticks the
  hazards that apply (14 groundworks/civils hazards in
  rams_library.py, each with standard controls), and adds
  notes. POST /api/rams/draft sends that to the AI, which
  returns method steps, risk rows (initial and residual
  severity/likelihood, 1–5) and a PPE list as JSON.
- The draft opens in an editor: every field editable, risk
  rows with severity/likelihood selects and live risk bands
  (5×5 matrix: Low ≤4, Medium 5–9, High 10–16, Very high
  17–25), add/remove hazard rows. Nothing saves until the
  user taps Save — the draft is never used as-is, and the UI
  and the printout both say so.
- Saved RAMS live in data/rams.json (same file-store pattern
  as saved articles, cap 200). List, open, edit, delete.
  "🖨️ Print / save as PDF" builds a clean print document
  (header, method statement, risk table, PPE, sign-off table
  for prepared/checked/site manager) and prints only that.
- Provider chain follows the chat hub: OpenRouter
  (deepseek/deepseek-chat) first, OpenAI (gpt-4o-mini) as
  fallback. Draft/save/update/delete are PIN-gated; the
  hazard library and the list are open reads.
