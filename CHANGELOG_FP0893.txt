Kaivor FP089.3 — Stock photos: distinctive words only
=====================================================

WHAT CHANGED (found in FP089.2's live check, 15 Nov 2026)
---------------------------------------------------------
FP089.2 fixed the bulldog and the fighter jet, but the live
check still found 2 wrong photos in the first 20 cards:
- a black-and-white torso on "Fanttik's S1 Pro electric
  screwdriver is $40 off in the run-up to Black Friday"
- a TV cartoon on "NHL Tonight: What to watch in Sunday's
  five showdowns"

DIAGNOSIS (verified against the live endpoint and Openverse)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Both photos matched GENERIC headline words. The torso is tagged
"black", "friday" and "pro" — three keyword hits. The cartoon
is titled "What are you watching tonight: Sunday show, NFL or
#Emmys?" — "tonight" and "sunday" matched, and "NFL" matched
the Sport category wordlist even though the photo is about TV,
not hockey. The scoring was fine; the WORDS were the problem.

HOW IT WORKS NOW
----------------
- Queries and scoring both use only the DISTINCTIVE words of a
  headline. Generic words are dropped entirely: days of the
  week, time words (today, tonight, tomorrow, weekend, ...),
  colours (a black-and-white photo is not a photo about
  "black"), number words, and weak verbs and fillers ("pro",
  "off", "run", "follow", "live", "watch", "show", ...).
  Possessives are normalised first ("Sunday's" is the day
  "sunday"). "new" stays in queries (dropping it would break
  "New Zealand" / "New York") but still does not score.
- Two-letter words count as keywords (AI, UK, US, TV are
  distinctive); the two-letter junk ("go", "up", "do", "so",
  "no") is stopworded.
- The single-word fallback prefers an all-caps acronym (NHL,
  NFL) when there is one, else the longest word ("screwdriver"
  beats the niche brand name "Fanttik's").
- A bare league acronym in a title or tag is no longer enough
  on its own for a gated category: it needs a keyword match
  alongside it. A full topical word ("football", "rugby",
  "phone") still passes on its own, and two keyword matches
  still pass on their own.
- Category wordlists pruned of words that are common in
  unrelated photo tags: "digital", "machine", "cloud" (Tech);
  "game", "cup", "belt", "ring" (Sport); "star" (Science).
  "computing", "server"/"servers" and "tournament" added.
