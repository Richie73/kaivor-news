Kaivor FP089.2 — Stock photos that match their story
======================================================

WHAT CHANGED (found live 15 Nov 2026, from Richie's screenshots)
----------------------------------------------------------------
FP089.1 made photos appear, but the wrong ones: a sepia bulldog
on an NFL story ("Follow live: Lions, Patriots face off in
Munich") and a fighter jet on a college football story ("CFP top
12 projection: Flawed Texas finding a way"). The lookup took
Openverse's first result for a short query with no relevance
check — "Patriots" alone returns patriotic archive photos, and
"Flawed Texas" returns a jet photographed in Fort Worth.

HOW IT WORKS NOW
----------------
- Each query now fetches 5 candidates (was 1) and scores them
  against the headline: a photo's title, tags and description
  are checked for headline keywords as WHOLE words (a trailing
  "s" is forgiven, so "lions" matches a photo titled "lion").
  Substring matching is deliberately NOT used — "patriots" must
  not match a photo titled "patriotic" (that slip is how the
  bulldog got through).
- Generic words do not count toward a match ("new", "way",
  "top", "finding", "year", "time", "man", "people", ...).
- The article's category gates the result. For Sport,
  Technology, Android, Science and Business a photo must match
  a topical word for that category (a small wordlist per
  category, e.g. football/stadium/match for Sport) or match two
  or more headline keywords — one keyword alone is not enough
  (a lion in Egypt is not the Detroit Lions). World, UK and
  Culture are too broad to gate, so they need one keyword match.
- The best-scoring passing photo wins. If nothing passes, the
  card stays text-only: no photo beats a wrong photo.
- The category travels from the card (data-category) through the
  page's fetch (?category=) to the /api/stock_image route, and
  is part of the cache key.

ALSO FIXED IN THIS MILESTONE
----------------------------
The 16th day of the forecast could show a ❓ icon with 0° / 0°
(found in FP091's live check): Open-Meteo's 16th day can arrive
with null values. renderWeatherDays now skips days with no
temperature data instead of rendering the broken row.
