FP121 — Today's tasks in the Hub and the morning brief
======================================================

What it does
------------
The Hub gains a "Tasks today" card showing open tasks due
today or overdue (up to 5, overdue first, then by due time),
each tickable in place with a link through to the full Tasks
view. The 06:00 morning brief lists the same tasks in a
"Tasks today:" section, so the day's work is visible before
the FP118 alerts start firing.

Design notes
------------
- Hub card sits between the Today (calendar) card and the
  Travel card, matching the calendar card's locked-state
  message ("Unlock Reading to see today's tasks.").
- The Hub reuses the existing toggleTask() from FP114 for
  the checkbox and task-due-overdue (FP118) for overdue
  titles. Dates use the phone's local date, not UTC, so
  the card is right in BST as well as GMT.
- The brief side is pure: brief_task_lines(tasks, today)
  in morning_brief.py, wired into build_morning_brief and
  format_brief_text. _build_live_brief() in main.py reads
  the task store directly (the brief route is cron-token
  gated, like the calendar fetch it sits beside); a store
  failure degrades to an empty task list, never a failed
  brief. Undated tasks are left out — they are not
  day-specific.
- No new env var, no migration, no new route.
