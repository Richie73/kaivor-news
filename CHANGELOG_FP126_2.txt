FP126.2 — Independent and Waffle puzzles launch their apps
===========================================================

Why
---
Richie reported (19 Nov, screenshot) that the Independent card
opened the puzzles page in the browser even though the app is
installed. Diagnosis: the Independent app claims no
independent.co.uk links at all — its assetlinks file lists
only uk.co.independent.bulletin for /bulletin/* — so a VIEW
intent for /extras/puzzles can never resolve and Chrome always
uses the browser fallback. The package name was verified
correct (com.freerange360.mpp.indy, live Play Store listing,
updated Sep 2026); the app itself is the blocker, not the link.

The same assetlinks check covered every other puzzle app:
Guardian, NYT Games, Nerdle, Sudoku.com, Lichess and Worldle
all verify their domains for all paths, so their VIEW intents
stand. Waffle is the other exception: assetlinks verifies
net.wafflegame.twa, not the Play Store app
(net.wafflegame.waffle), so its VIEW intent is unreliable —
and the app opens straight into the game anyway, so launching
it loses nothing.

Changes
-------
- New main.launch_link(web_url, package): an intent that opens
  an app's home screen (action MAIN + category LAUNCHER, no
  URL data), with the same browser fallback when the app is
  missing.
- New PUZZLE_LAUNCH_PACKAGES map: The Independent and Waffle.
  puzzle_items() gives them a launch intent; the other six
  sources keep VIEW intents.
- Tests: the FP126 file splits its expectations into VIEW and
  launch maps, adds an exact-string launch test and a launch
  test per puzzle, and the dedupe/feed-gate survival tests
  cover both kinds.
