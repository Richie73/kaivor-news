Kaivor FP089.1 — Stock photo fallback that actually finds photos
================================================================

WHAT CHANGED (fix for FP089, found live 15 Nov 2026)
----------------------------------------------------
FP089 deployed and its API endpoint worked, but almost no card
gained a photo: the five-word keyword query returned zero results
from Openverse for most headlines. Verified live: "We've waited
years here Wales" -> 0 results, while "Wales New Zealand" -> 240
and "Zealand" -> 240. The page wiring was fine (the placeholder
appeared, then vanished when the lookup missed) — the queries were
too specific.

HOW IT WORKS
------------
- get_stock_image now tries a chain of candidate queries and takes
  the first hit: (1) the full five-word set, (2) the proper nouns
  (capitalised words other than the first, which is capitalised by
  headline convention), (3) the single most distinctive word
  (longest proper noun, else longest word). At most three
  Openverse calls per headline.
- keywords_from_headline now returns the word list (was the joined
  string) so the chain can build its candidates. Leading/trailing
  quote marks are stripped ("here'" -> "here"); internal
  apostrophes stay ("We've").
- "new" is no longer a stopword: dropping it broke "New Zealand",
  "New York" and other proper names. A weak word is harmless now
  because the chain falls back.
- Caching: each attempt is cached under its own query, and the
  final result is also cached under the headline's full query, so
  a repeat headline costs no new lookup. Failures still retry
  after 60s.
- Also fixed in this milestone: some feeds carry the literal text
  "null"/"None" for a missing summary (seen on ESPN live-blog
  entries, 15 Nov 2026). normalise_article now treats those as no
  description instead of showing the word.
