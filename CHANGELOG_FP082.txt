Kaivor FP082 — Weather redesign: slim strip + full Weather category
================================================================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
The tall weather card that pushed the news down the home page is
split in two:

1. SLIM STRIP ON HOME. One line under the category chips: place,
   temperature, condition and today's one-line summary ("Dry until
   14:00, then rain."). Tap it to open the full forecast.

2. FULL WEATHER AS ITS OWN CATEGORY. A new 🌤️ Weather chip sits
   in the category rail after Puzzles (and in the swipe order).
   The full view is polished:
   - Hero: big temperature, condition icon, feels-like, today's
     high and low.
   - Details grid: wind (with gusts), humidity, rain, and
     sunrise/sunset (new — the forecast request now asks Open-Meteo
     for sunrise and sunset).
   - The one-line summary and the 12-hour strip, as before.
   - The 7-day forecast is now a vertical list — one full-width
     row per day (name, icon, rain chance, high/low) — instead of
     a horizontal strip.
   - Search and recent places, as before.

NAVIGATION
----------
Opening Weather hides the news cards WITHOUT destroying them, so
going back to any news category just shows them again. (Chat and
Saved replace the news list instead — Weather deliberately does
not.) Every other view — news categories, Saved, Relevance, Chat —
hides the full weather view again and brings the strip back.

TECHNICAL
---------
- templates/index.html only (plus tests and docs). No Python, no
  server route, no new keys, no cost — still Open-Meteo, free.
- The forecast request adds sunrise,sunset to the daily fields.
- New pure helper formatSunTime (ISO "2026-11-14T07:12" -> "07:12"),
  covered by a node logic test like the other weather helpers.
- The FP073/FP074 tests are updated where the design changed: the
  FP073 daily-fields assertion now includes sunrise,sunset, and
  the FP074 days test now asserts the vertical list.
- The old .weather-now-main / .weather-icon / .weather-temp /
  .weather-day strip styles are removed; nothing else used them.
