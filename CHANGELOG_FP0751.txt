Kaivor FP075.1 — Sharper pictures: BBC thumbnails upgraded
==========================================================

WHAT CHANGED (reported by Richie, 15 Nov 2026)
----------------------------------------------
"The pictures that are populated are a bit blurry."

DIAGNOSIS (measured live, 15 Nov 2026)
--------------------------------------
Every card image on the live site was downloaded and measured:
- BBC (ichef.bbci.co.uk): 240x135 — the biggest image source
  in the feed (96 of 400 cards) and by far the smallest. A
  240px image stretched across a card is the blurriness.
- Guardian (i.guim.co.uk): 700x560 — acceptable.
- NYT: 1800x1800. Independent: 1200x800. Sky: 1920x1080.
  Ars Technica: 1024x648. Stock photos (Openverse): 1024px
  on the long edge. All fine.

HOW IT WORKS NOW
----------------
The BBC's image service takes the width in the URL, so
_clean_image_url in news_store.py (the choke point every
article's image passes through) rewrites the width to 1024:
  .../ace/standard/240/... -> .../ace/standard/1024/...
  .../news/320/...         -> .../news/1024/...
Verified live: the same BBC image returns 1024x576 (148 KB
instead of 10 KB). The Guardian's URLs carry a signed hash,
so they cannot be rewritten the same way — they stay at the
700px the API gives, which is acceptable.
