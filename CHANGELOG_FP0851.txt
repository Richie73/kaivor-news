Kaivor FP085.1 — Tapping News after Saved, Chat or Settings brings the stories back
====================================================================================

THE BUG
-------
The Saved, Chat and Settings views replace the news cards in
#articles-container. Tapping a news category afterwards only
showed/hid the cards then in the container — so tapping News left
the view in place (its cards match "All"), and tapping a single
category left the list blank. Only a full page reload brought the
stories back. Found by the FP085 live check (14 Nov 2026): after
opening Settings, tapping News twice did nothing.

THE FIX
-------
- cacheNewsContainer() saves the news HTML before a view replaces
  it. A flag (newsViewReplaced) stops one view caching another
  view as "news" — the Saved view's cards look like news cards.
- restoreNewsContainer() puts the news HTML back when a news
  category is picked (called first in filterCategory, before the
  cards are collected). It also re-syncs each card's Save button
  from savedIds, in case a story was unsaved while the Saved view
  was open.
- The news is only ever re-rendered by a full page reload, which
  resets this state, so the cache cannot go stale.
- One fix covers all three views (Saved, Chat, Settings) plus the
  #settings / #chat / #saved deep links, which open the same
  views.
