Kaivor FP099 — Voice input in chat
================================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
The chat form gains a 🎤 microphone button. It uses the browser's
built-in speech recognition (Chrome on Android) — free, no key,
no server round-trip, no new cost.

- Tap 🎤 to start listening (the button turns red and shows ⏹);
  tap again to stop. Words appear in the input as you speak
  (interim results); the transcript lands in the input and you
  tap Send as normal — nothing is ever sent automatically.
- Language is en-GB. Anything already typed stays, with the
  spoken words added after it.
- If the browser has no speech recognition, the button hides
  itself. If the mic is blocked, or nothing is heard, or the
  connection drops, a short note appears in the chat.
- The mic stops when you leave the chat view (both
  cacheNewsContainer and restoreNewsContainer stop it), so it
  can never keep listening on another screen.

Template-only change — no Python, no routes, no keys.
