FP117 — One green across the whole app
=====================================

The app had grown a dozen different greens: emerald #10b981,
#34d399, #22c55e, mint #a7f3d0, pale #d1fae5, dark #059669 /
#064e3b, plus a scatter of hardcoded teal-greens in the AI
drawer, timeline, chat bubbles and market flashes. They all
clashed with the Spark K brand green.

Now there is one green: the brand green #35FA33.

- Every accent green is exactly #35FA33: the tokens --green,
  --green-bright, --green-soft, --green-pale, --green-dark
  (index) and --accent (Reading) all point at it, and the
  hardcoded accents (chat toggles, RAMS checkboxes, chat
  source links, the AI section heading, the timeline "Open
  report" link, the market price-up text and flash glow) now
  use var(--brand) or the brand hex.
- Dark surfaces and muted text are tints of that same hue
  (120 degrees: red == blue, green dominant), so nothing
  clashes and nothing is unreadable — pure neon on every
  background would have been. New token values: --green-panel
  #0f1a0f, --green-border #1f3d1f, --btn-bg #1c2b1c,
  --btn-border #264026, --green-darker #0f2a0f; the AI drawer,
  chat bubble, badges, timeline and muted text use the same
  family (#1a4d1a, #111a11, #0f150f, #d6e8d6, #c2d2c2,
  #eaf6ea, #a8bfa8, #9db29d, #b3c6b3, #8a9a8a).
- New token --on-brand #071207 (both templates): the dark text
  on bright green buttons — the Save button, the scroll-to-top
  button and Reading's primary button. The Save button is now
  a bright brand-green button with dark text; its saved state
  is the dark tint with a brand border.

Tests: 7 in tests/test_one_green_fp117.py (accent tokens are
the brand green, dark tints are the brand hue, Reading accent,
no old green anywhere, a whitelist scan proving every greenish
hex in either template is one of the allowed values, bright
buttons use dark text, price-up uses the brand + panel tokens).
tests/test_design_system_fp090.py token assertions updated to
the new values.
