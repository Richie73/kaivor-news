FP111.1: The header no longer hides the top of the page
=====================================================
Date: 16 Nov 2026. Richie reported "The market box looks odd"
with a screenshot: the "MARKET WATCH" heading was sliced in half
at the top of the screen.

Root cause (pre-existing, exposed by FP111): the header was
position:fixed and ~107px tall (logo + tagline + Settings
button + padding), but the body only cleared 70px — so the top
~37px of the page sat permanently under the header. The market
panel is the first element, so its heading took the hit. Before
FP111 the panel's head row was taller (it held the refresh
button), so the heading poked below the hidden zone; removing
the button shrank the row and the heading slid fully into it.

Fix: the header is now position:sticky (top:0, same z-index and
background, so scrolled content still slides under it) and the
body's top padding is 0. Sticky keeps the header pinned while
scrolling but takes its own space in the layout, so no magic
padding number is needed and the fix holds under any font
scaling. Safe here: no ancestor of the header sets overflow
(verified by test). The bottom nav is still fixed, so the
body's 64px bottom padding stays.

Tests: 3 in tests/test_sticky_header_fp111.py (sticky + top +
z-index/background; body padding; no overflow ancestor). The
FP090 design-system test's exact header-rule assertion updated
(fixed -> sticky). Local: 883 passed / 11 known failures /
2 skipped. Phone expected: 894 passed / 0 failed / 2 skipped.

Files: templates/index.html, tests/test_sticky_header_fp111.py,
tests/test_design_system_fp090.py.
