FP068 — Freedium integration and Freedium publishers' news feeds

Slice 1 — Freedium Mirror fallback for article extraction
- When direct extraction fails with extraction_failed on a Freedium-supported
  publisher domain (Medium, NYT, Washington Post, Bloomberg, Reuters, The
  Economist, FT), the extractor retries once through the Freedium Mirror
  (https://freedium-mirror.cfd/<article-url>).
- The mirror attempt runs through the same validated fetch path: SSRF checks
  on every URL, 4-redirect limit, 1.5 MB size cap, same timeouts, same
  quality gates. A failed mirror attempt returns the original direct result,
  so a mirror outage costs nothing.
- Mirror successes are marked content_source "freedium_mirror" and
  via_freedium true; the AI brief retrieval record now carries via_freedium
  and the content_source allowlist in main.py accepts freedium_mirror.
- HTTP errors are deliberately not retried: a publisher that actively
  refuses the request is not hammered through a mirror.
- Added "prose" to the article-container markers (the class the mirror
  renders the article body in); the mirror's <article> inside <main> was
  already matched. Verified live 12 Nov 2026: the mirror renders a Medium
  article's full text inside <article>/<main>.

Slice 2 — "Unlock via Freedium" link in the news UI
- publisher_access.py now exposes freedium_url_for() and
  freedium_access_info(); main.py passes article_freedium_access() to the
  news template, which renders an "Unlock via Freedium" link for supported
  articles. The NYT subscription link stays primary; Freedium is the
  fallback label. publisher_access_info() behaviour is unchanged.

Slice 3 — Freedium publishers' feeds in the matching news categories
- 13 feeds added to DEFAULT_FEEDS in news_freshness.py (48 -> 61 feeds):
  World +3 (Washington Post, Financial Times, The Economist International),
  Technology +3 (Washington Post, Financial Times, Bloomberg),
  Business +4 (Bloomberg Markets, Washington Post, Financial Times
  Companies, The Economist Finance and Economics), Science +2 (NYT Science,
  The Economist Science and Technology), UK +1 (The Economist Britain).
- No additions for Sport, Android or Culture — no fitting feeds.
- Reuters is deliberately left out: Reuters has no working public RSS feed
  (confirmed Oct 2026; direct RSS removed in 2020). Reuters links still get
  the slice-1 fallback when they arrive via NewsData.io or the Guardian API.
- Medium is deliberately left out: Medium has no editorial feed, only noisy
  community tag feeds. Medium links still get the slice-1 fallback.
