Kaivor FP101 — Morning hub
=========================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
A sixth bottom-nav destination, 🌅 Hub (first in the bar, and on
the Reading page too, linking to /#hub): one place to start the
day.

- A time-aware greeting ("Good morning, Richie" etc.) and the
  date in full.
- The weather now — reusing the last forecast already fetched
  (renderWeather stores it in weatherLastNow), so no second
  call: conditions, temperature, feels-like, today's high/low
  and the rain summary. Before the first forecast loads, the
  hub says the weather is still loading.
- The top five stories, read from the news cards already on
  the page (or the cached news HTML when another view has
  replaced the container) — no second fetch.
- A market snapshot — Gold, Bitcoin and £ → $ — from
  /api/ticker, reusing the markets price formatter. If the
  price store is empty (after a restart), the hub points at
  the Markets view instead of showing blanks.

Calendar and live travel are NOT in v1 — they need the Google
account connected to Kaivor, which is a bigger job.

The hub hides the news cards without destroying them, like the
weather and markets views, so every other view hides it
(filterCategory, Saved, Chat, Settings, Weather, Markets), and
opening the hub hides Weather and Markets. Deep link #hub works
on load.

Template-only change — no Python, no routes, no keys.
