Kaivor FP089.4 — Stock photos: two-letter keywords allowlisted
================================================================

WHAT CHANGED (found in FP089.3's live check, 15 Nov 2026)
---------------------------------------------------------
FP089.3's live check found 7 of 8 illustrative photos on-topic
and both named failures fixed (screwdriver story: screwdriver
photo; NHL story: hockey photo). One fail remained: a Levi's
wall mural on "Coaches under pressure, fiery AD statements and
Utah comes up clutch in Week 11".

DIAGNOSIS (verified against the live endpoint and Openverse)
-----------------------------------------------------------
The mural is titled "Levi's Ad, Utah". "AD" in the headline
means Athletic Director; "ad" in the photo's title means
advertisement. "ad" plus "utah" gave the mural two keyword
hits, which passes the Sport gate. A two-letter homonym
collision — the same class of problem as "PM" (Prime Minister
or afternoon) and "MP" (Member of Parliament or megapixels).

HOW IT WORKS NOW
----------------
Two-letter words only count as keywords when they are in the
allowlist _TWO_LETTER_KEYWORDS: AI, UK, US, TV, EU, UN. Every
other two-letter word is dropped from the queries and the
scoring. (FP089.3 had let all two-letter words through.)

LIVE DRY-RUN (working copy against the real Openverse, 15 Nov)
--------------------------------------------------------------
The Utah story now gets NO PHOTO: the top results for "Utah"
are Salt Lake City and St. George scenery, correctly rejected
by the Sport gate (one keyword, no sport word). The mocked
test proves an Olympic torch photo in Utah would win when it
appears in a fetched page (keyword "utah" plus category word
"olympic").
