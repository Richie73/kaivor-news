Kaivor FP097 — Splash lets its animation finish
=============================================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
The splash already faded out smoothly (FP083), but it hid 900ms
after the page parsed — before the shine sweep (1.4s + 0.45s
delay = 1.85s) had finished, so the animation was cut off
mid-sweep and the logo never got its moment.

hideSplash now waits for a 2s minimum display: an early call
reschedules itself for the remaining time instead of hiding,
and only one reschedule is ever queued. The hard 3s timeout
still caps the splash on a slow load, and the 0.45s fade-out
is unchanged.
