Kaivor FP098 — Two live-check fixes
=================================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
Two real bugs found by the FP092–FP097 live check on 15 Nov 2026
(both pre-existing, neither from the polish round):

1. A literal "&nbsp;" showed in a card description. Some feeds
   send HTML entities as literal text; the templates escape on
   render, so "&nbsp;" became "&amp;nbsp;" and showed on screen.
   normalise_article now decodes entities once (titles and
   descriptions) via a new _decode_entities helper in
   news_store.py; the template re-escapes anything that needs it
   on the way out. Non-breaking spaces become regular spaces.

2. The "why it matters" significance line often mismatched the
   story — Russell Brand stories said "This is primarily a
   motorsport development". Two causes in
   story_intelligence._impact_hint:
   (a) keywords matched as substrings, so "ai" hit "said" and
       "race" hit "grace". Keywords now match as whole
       words/phrases (_keyword_hit with \b boundaries); the
       plurals the substring test used to cover for free
       (wars, attacks, drones, chips, trials, vaccines, laws,
       banks) are listed explicitly.
   (b) the motorsport rule listed driver surnames, so "russell"
       matched Russell Brand. Surnames and team names
       (_MOTORSPORT_NAMES) now only count alongside a
       motorsport context word (_MOTORSPORT_CONTEXT: race,
       racing, driver, circuit, lap, podium, grid, pit,
       championship). "Russell takes pole position" still
       matches via the anchor phrases; "Verstappen wins the
       race" matches via surname + context.
   The uncertainty rules are intentionally unchanged — their
   keywords are stems by design ("casualt", "injur").
