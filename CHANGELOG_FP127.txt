FP127 — Football in the morning brief
====================================

Why
---
Richie picked this as the next Kaivor upgrade (19 Nov): the
06:00 brief lists today's fixtures and last night's scores,
from the FP110 live scores feed (Premier League + England).

Changes
-------
- morning_brief.brief_football_lines(scores, today): pure
  builder. Live matches first with their clock ("Live 67' —
  Liverpool 1–0 Man City"), then today's fixtures by kickoff
  ("Today 17:30 — Arsenal v Chelsea"), then yesterday's
  finished games ("Last night — Liverpool 2–1 Man City").
  Kickoffs convert to London time (GMT/BST). Caps: 5
  fixtures, 4 results. Anything missing, unparseable or
  outside today/yesterday is skipped; a failed feed returns
  no lines, never a broken brief.
- build_morning_brief gains an optional football param;
  format_brief_text adds a "Football:" section after Tasks,
  before Travel. The JSON brief carries the same lines.
- main._build_live_brief fetches get_scores() in try/except
  (same degrade pattern as tasks) and passes it through.

Honest caveat: ESPN's scoreboard covers the current
gameweek, so last night's results only appear while the feed
still carries them. On a weekday morning after a weekend
round, the results may already have dropped off — the
section then just shows the coming fixtures, or nothing at
all when there is no football.

Files
-----
