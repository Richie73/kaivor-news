FP124 — Rain radar provider switch: RainViewer → LibreWXR
=========================================================

Why
---
RainViewer cut its free tier to zoom 7 on 1 Jan 2026 — that
was the "Zoom Level Not Supported" bug FP119.1 patched around.
Richie asked for more detail and mentioned his Weawow and
Windfinder Pro subscriptions; neither offers a developer API
(Weawow's own radar IS RainViewer), Windy's embed is €990/yr,
OpenWeatherMap's radar also caps at zoom 7, and the Met Office
DataHub has no live radar at all. LibreWXR is a free,
open-source RainViewer-compatible API with a live public
instance: same v2 API format, no key, tiles to zoom 12, plus
nowcast frames RainViewer no longer gives free users.

Verified before switching (18 Nov)
----------------------------------
- Feed: 12 past + 6 nowcast frames, CORS `*` on feed and tiles.
- Tiles over Puddington: z7 49 KB, z8 42 KB real radar; z10
  London 29 KB real radar. (Puddington z10/z12 were 334-byte
  empty tiles — no rain overhead at the time, not a
  placeholder: the London tile at the same zoom differs.)
- Tile content varies by location at every zoom tested —
  the FP119.1 lesson applied (compare content, not status).

Changes
-------
- Maps feed: api.rainviewer.com → api.librewxr.net
  (constant renamed RAINVIEWER_MAPS_URL → RADAR_MAPS_URL).
- Tile host: tilecache.rainviewer.com → api.librewxr.net
  (rainRadarTileUrl, the frame setUrl, the Leaflet layer).
- Attribution: RainViewer → LibreWXR (librewxr.net).
- Zoom: default 7 → 8, cap 7 → 10. LibreWXR serves to 12,
  but the underlying data is coarse (~2–5 km), so past 10
  there is no more real detail.

Honest caveat
-------------
LibreWXR is a small open-source project, not a big company —
less guaranteed than a paid service. If it ever goes flaky,
the fallback is RainViewer (zoom 7) or Rainbow's Tiles API
(free tier, ~1km, needs a key + server proxy).
