Kaivor FP092 — Faster news list
==============================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
The news page renders every story into the DOM (600+ on a full
feed), which is heavy on a phone. Two changes:

1. Pagination: only the first 50 stories show. A "Load more
   stories" button under the list reveals the next 50, with a
   "Showing X of Y stories" count. The button hides itself when
   every matching story is shown. Picking a category starts back
   at the first 50. The Saved, Chat, Settings, Weather and
   Markets views hide the button while they are open.
2. Cards get content-visibility, so the browser skips painting
   off-screen cards until they scroll into view.

Matching is computed from the current category, not from display
state: filterCategory resets display on every call, but
loadMoreNews does not, so reading display would count only the
cards already visible. Skeleton cards are never counted.
