Kaivor FP086.2 — Markets strip status updates at once
===================================================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
FP086.1's live check found one real gap: after the Markets view
filled the strip's prices, the status text beside MARKET WATCH
still read "No live data yet — tap Refresh markets". The fill set
the flag the 30-second ticker poll reads, but the status only
changed on the next poll — up to 30 seconds later — so the check
caught it still saying the old text while prices were showing.

Fix: fillTickerFromHistory now calls setMarketStatus itself the
moment the strip fills ("Prices from the Markets view · Updated
0s ago"), and the poll keeps the age fresh from there. One line
of behaviour, in templates/index.html only.
