FP109: Exclude American-football and baseball stories from the news feed
======================================================================
Date: 16 Nov 2026. Requested by Richie: no news stories relating to
American football or baseball.

What it does
------------
Every refreshed article (RSS, Guardian, NewsData) passes through
news_store.deduplicate_articles(), so the filter lives there: one hook
covers all sources. Articles matching the filter are dropped at refresh
time, before they reach the news cache — so the homepage, the Sport view,
the Morning Hub top stories and the morning brief are all clean.

Matching (news_store.is_us_sport_story)
---------------------------------------
Whole-word/phrase match on title + description (case-insensitive):
  nfl, super bowl, american football, college football,
  quarterback(s), gridiron, mlb, world series, baseball(s)
Plus league URL paths (ESPN/BBC/Guardian/Sky carry them even when the
headline does not name the league):
  /nfl/, /mlb/, /american-football/, /baseball/

Deliberately NOT matched
------------------------
- "touchdown": also used for spacecraft landings.
- "home run", "pitcher": common outside baseball.
- Basketball/NBA: Richie did not ask to exclude it; it stays.

Known trade-off
---------------
"world series" also drops the occasional World Series of Darts/Poker
story. The MLB World Series is baseball's biggest event, so the term
stays; the darts cost is a handful of stories a year.

Saved articles are NOT filtered: load_saved_articles does not go through
deduplicate_articles, so anything Richie already saved stays saved.

Measured before the build (16 Nov): the live homepage carried 142
mentions of "NFL" and 5 of "MLB", almost all from the general ESPN feed
(espn.com/nfl/... and espn.com/mlb/... links). The ESPN feed itself is
kept — its football (soccer) stories still come through.
