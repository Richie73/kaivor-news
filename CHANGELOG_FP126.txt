FP126 — Puzzle cards open the puzzle's own Android app
=========================================================

Why
---
Richie reported (19 Nov) that only the NYT Wordle card opens
its app — the other puzzles open as web links. Wordle works
because the NYT Games app claims nytimes.com links (App
Links); the other publishers' links have no such routing.

Changes
-------
- New main.app_link(web_url, package): builds an Android
  intent URL (intent://host/path#Intent;scheme=https;
  package=<pkg>;S.browser_fallback_url=<encoded web URL>;end).
  Tapping it opens the app when installed; Chrome falls back
  to the web page when it is not, so a missing app degrades
  to the old behaviour instead of an error.
- New PUZZLE_APP_PACKAGES map (source -> official Android
  package, each verified against Google Play on 19 Nov 2026):
  NYT Games com.nytimes.crossword (Wordle, Mini, Connections,
  Strands), Guardian com.guardian, Independent
  com.freerange360.mpp.indy, Nerdle com.nerdle.app, Sudoku.com
  com.easybrain.sudoku.android, Lichess org.lichess.mobileV2
  (the current app; org.lichess.mobileapp is the legacy one),
  Worldle fr.teuteuf.worldle.twa, Waffle net.wafflegame.waffle.
- puzzle_items() rewrites each mapped puzzle's link to its
  intent URL. Both card renderers (server Jinja + client JS)
  use article.link, so both pick it up with no renderer
  changes beyond the label.
- Quordle deliberately keeps its plain web link:
  Merriam-Webster has no official Quordle app (web-only) and
  the Play Store results are copycats.
- Card button label is now intent-aware in both renderers:
  "Open in app" for intent links, "Open in Chrome" otherwise
  (so Quordle still says Chrome).
- Honest caveat: an intent opens the app, but whether it
  lands on the exact puzzle or the app's home screen depends
  on the app's own deep-link handling — the NYT, Guardian,
  Nerdle and Worldle apps handle their URLs; others may open
  at home. Nothing here can force an app to a screen it does
  not expose.
